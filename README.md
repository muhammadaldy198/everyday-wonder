One sentence, 159 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life:

> The photo strip in my wallet, creased from fifteen years of pockets, shows four faces from 2011, and the third one is smiling at something I can no longer see.

## How it was made

The sentence is built on one concrete object — a photo strip, creased from fifteen years of pockets — and one turn: the third face is still smiling at something the holder can no longer see. The program writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` exactly as written, with no trailing newline.
